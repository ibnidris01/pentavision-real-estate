Who We Are

Pentavision's mission is to redefine Nigeria's real-estate investment experience with a bold 5-Point agenda: eradicate fear, deliver unmatched ROI, enhance lifestyles, drive economic growth, and foster trust through education and integrity.

As a mentee of Dr. Olumide Emmanuel and a protégé of Dr. Tope Mark Odigie, founders of the most reliable real estate company in Africa, Peace has excelled in advertising, sales, and client relations, helping clients secure optimal investments and preparing key land-acquisition documents.

She earned the Top Selling Realtor award in 2022 while at Reb360 where she identified a gap in real-estate education, inspiring Pentavision to empower investors with knowledge.

Our core values include Security, Transparency, Integrity, Comfort, Youth empowerment, and Innovation, leveraging cutting-edge tech like AI.

Pentavision was founded on January 10th, 2025 and has contributed to developing 9 estates across Nigeria.

Our Mission

To transform the Nigerian real estate landscape by eliminating fraud, land disputes, and allocation delays . We deliver verified, legally-backed investments that protect our clients and set a new standard for trust and integrity in the industry.

Our Vision

We envision a future where the Nigerian diaspora can invest in Nigeria’s real estate with confidence. Our commitment is to protect investor funds and assets, secure legal ownership free from risk, and provide hospitality-driven properties that appreciate over time and deliver consistent returns. We bridge the gap between the diaspora and home, turning real estate into a trusted vehicle for wealth creation.
